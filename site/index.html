01 · Features

Training that feels like the game

Same sens, same crosshair, same keys, same movement rules. What you practise here carries straight over to your next match.

Settings import

Plays with your VALORANT settings

ValTrainer reads VALORANT's settings files each time it starts. It only reads them, never changes them, and works the same whether VALORANT is running or not.

  • Aim: sensitivity plus scoped and ADS multipliers, with VALORANT's math (0.07° per count × sens, 103° horizontal FOV) and raw mouse input, so your cm/360 is identical.
  • Look: your crosshair profile, pixel for pixel, with movement and firing error, and your enemy highlight colour.
  • Controls: movement, walk, crouch, jump and ability keybinds, and hold or toggle scope.
  • Display: resolution, display mode, monitor, VSync and FPS cap.

No VALORANT on this PC? ValTrainer uses VALORANT's defaults and you set your sens in Settings. Several accounts on one PC? Pick one in Settings.

Aim coach

An aim coach that finds what holds you back

Every run records your mouse input, crosshair path and shots on your PC. The coach analyses them and tells you, in plain words, what to fix next.

  • An estimated rank, overall and for 8 skills: flicking, precision, tracking, reaction, crosshair placement, spray, movement and utility, each with a confidence level.
  • Problems sorted by importance, each with your numbers, why it happens, how to fix it and drills to practise: over-flicking, missing micro-adjustments, clicking while still moving, a low crosshair, late spray pull-down…
  • A run review on every results screen: what to fix next run, and what went well.
  • Sensitivity advice that changes by at most 18% at a time and stays within 20–80 cm/360.
New in 1.4

Agent training for all 29 agents

The Agents screen groups every agent by role. Each agent page shows their abilities, which drill trains each one, signature drills played with that agent's own utility, drills for their role, and a 10-minute agent warm-up.

  • Flash & Peek: throw your own flash (Phoenix, Skye, Yoru, KAY/O, Breach, Gekko) or nearsight (Reyna, Omen), then swing and kill the blinded defenders.
  • Smoke Execute for Brimstone, Omen, Astra, Clove, Viper, Harbor and Miks: plan on a top-down map, smoke with the agent's own mechanic and take the site. Scored on the sightlines you cut, with a replay.
  • Recon & Clear (Sova, Fade), Mobility Entry (Jett, Neon, Raze, Reyna, Yoru, Waylay, Iso) and Chamber Guns.
  • Site Anchor: hold a site alone against an execute, with a sentinel setup you place yourself: Killjoy, Cypher, Deadlock, Vyse, Sage, Veto or Chamber.
Maps

Real maps at true scale

Map drills take place in blockouts of real map areas, traced at true scale from the official layouts and callout positions, with real cover, heights, defender spots, plant spots and the standard controller smokes.

  • Ascent
  • Bind
  • Breeze
  • Haven
  • Icebox
  • Split
  • Deathmatch: free-for-all against 3–6 bots at your tier. They roam, hunt you, hold angles and fight each other; the fallen drop health packs.
  • Site Clear, Post-Plant, Retake and Site Anchor: clear real chokes, hold after the plant, or defuse before the spike blows.
  • Flash Dodge and Sound Lock: turn away from real flashes with per-agent timings and sounds, and pre-aim from footsteps.
Recoil

Spray control you can see

The Vandal and Phantom follow Riot's documented recoil system: a vertical climb that plateaus, side-locked horizontal drift, spread that grows per bullet, and recovery after a tap or a full spray.

  • After every spray, a spray chart and coaching such as “pull down more” or “counter the drift right”.
  • Spray Transfer: kill every agent in one continuous spray.
  • Long-range Taps: 30–50 m Vandal taps, with a ring that shows when the recoil has reset.
  • Operator with a 2.5× scope and bolt action.

The per-bullet recoil numbers are reconstructed; Riot never published them.

Movement

Movement that works like the real thing

Running adds 6° of inaccuracy and walking 3°. You are accurate below 27.5% of run speed, which is what makes counter-strafing work, and stops match Riot's measured stop times.

  • Counter-Strafe, Peek Duels (the bot shoots back), Jiggle Peek (bait the Op, punish during his bolt) and Jump Peek.
  • Jumping: jump and crouch-jump onto ledges, air strafing, airborne and landing inaccuracy, and fall damage.
  • Bots that fight like people: a human-like reaction delay, then they move their crosshair onto you and fire with a per-shot hit chance against your 100 HP + 50 shield.
Warm up

Warm up like the pros

Pick Quick (≈8 min), Standard (≈15 min) or Pro (≈25 min). It runs drills at your tier from easy to hard: smooth tracking and big targets first, then flicks and target switching, then strafing bots, movement and peeks, then a deathmatch.

  • An optional sens shifter starts a little above or below your sens, then steps back. The screen is honest that there's little evidence for it either way.
  • A summary with a lock-in graph: each drill as a % of your 7-day average, start → end comparisons, and whether you're warmed up.
Coach tools

Find your sens and your crosshair

  • Sens Finder (about 13 minutes): you alternate between two unlabelled sensitivities with flicks and strafe tracking, and it narrows down to the one you score best with. You get a VALORANT sens, eDPI and cm/360.
  • Crosshair Finder (about 6 minutes): ranks colours and outlines by contrast against the maps and your enemy highlight colour, times how fast you spot each one, then runs a bracket of shapes decided by your aim. You get a VALORANT crosshair code to import.

ValTrainer never changes VALORANT. You apply the new sens or crosshair yourself in VALORANT's settings.

03 · Install

Up and running in a minute

No admin rights, no account, nothing else to install: .NET is bundled.

  1. Download

    • Setup.exe (recommended): installs for your Windows user, with Start menu shortcuts, an optional desktop icon and an uninstaller.
    • Portable.zip: no installation, unzip anywhere and run ValTrainer.exe.
    Get Setup.exe

  2. Windows warning?

    ValTrainer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC” about a new download. Click:

    More info→Run anyway

    The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.

  3. Check the file

    Optional: compare the SHA-256 hash with SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release. In PowerShell:

    Get-FileHash .\ValTrainer-1.4.0-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

  4. Play

    Your VALORANT settings are imported on the first start. Pick a tier and a drill.

    To get coach ratings, play Head Flicks, Strafe Tracking, Peek Practice, Counter-Strafe and Vandal Spray a few times each.

Other downloads: Portable.zip (176 MB) · SHA256SUMS.txt · All releases. Both versions share the same settings and stats in %APPDATA%\ValTrainer, so you can switch between them.

04 · Updates

Updates itself, like Discord or Steam

Install once. New drills and fixes arrive on their own, and your settings and stats are kept.

  1. ChecksAt startup and every 6 hours, ValTrainer asks GitHub whether a newer version is out.
  2. DownloadsIn the background, while you play. Downloads resume after a restart or a dropped connection.
  3. VerifiesEvery file is checked against the release's published SHA-256 hash. A damaged file is deleted.
  4. RestartsClick RESTART TO UPDATE or just keep playing: it installs the next time you start.
  • Installer version: the new Setup runs silently and upgrades in place.
  • Portable version: the new ValTrainer.exe replaces the old one in its folder.
  • You're in control: Settings → About has Check for updates, Download updates automatically and Check now. Skipped versions are never downloaded.
  • Installed with a version before 1.3.0? Run the new installer once by hand; after that it's automatic.

06 · FAQ and privacy

Questions, answered

Privacy

  • Nothing is uploaded. No account, no analytics, no telemetry leaves your PC.
  • Your settings, stats and run recordings stay in %APPDATA%\ValTrainer. Delete that folder to reset everything.
  • The only network traffic is the optional update check to api.github.com (at startup and every 6 hours) and downloading a newer release's files from GitHub. Turn it off in Settings and ValTrainer never goes online.

This website has no cookies, analytics, trackers or ads. To link the newest files, your browser asks api.github.com for the latest release once per session. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Is it safe? Does it touch VALORANT?

ValTrainer is a separate program that never interacts with the game. It never reads or writes VALORANT's memory, never injects anything, never automates or simulates input for the game, and never interacts with the VALORANT process or Riot Vanguard.

The only thing it does with VALORANT is read its settings files (%LOCALAPPDATA%\VALORANT\Saved\Config), the same text files VALORANT saves your settings in. It opens them read-only, never changes them, and works the same whether VALORANT is running or not.

Why does Windows warn me when I run it?

ValTrainer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns about new downloads. Click More info → Run anyway. To check that your download is the official one, compare its SHA-256 hash with SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release (see Install). The source code is public.

Does it work without VALORANT?

Yes. Without VALORANT, ValTrainer uses VALORANT's default settings and you set your sensitivity in Settings. You can also override any imported setting there.

Will it change my VALORANT settings?

No. The Sens Finder, the coach's sens advice and the Crosshair Finder give you a sensitivity or a crosshair code; you set it yourself in VALORANT's settings. “Use in ValTrainer” only changes ValTrainer.

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no account, no paid tier. ValTrainer is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (GPL-3.0-or-later).

Is it open source? Can I help?

Yes, all of it is on GitHub. Bug reports, drill ideas, tuning data and code are welcome: see CONTRIBUTING.md, open an issue, or give the feature requests you want a 👍.

Is it made by Riot Games?

No. ValTrainer is fan-made and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Riot Games. It uses no Riot art, audio or models. All 3D models, textures, skies and recorded sounds are CC0 (public domain); see the credits.

The game won't start or the screen stays black

Use the Start menu shortcut “ValTrainer (safe graphics)”, or run ValTrainer.exe --rendering-method gl_compatibility. In that mode the first start can freeze for up to about 30 s while shaders compile; later starts are fast. Still stuck? Open a bug report (Settings → About can copy your system info).

Mac or Linux?

ValTrainer is made for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only.
